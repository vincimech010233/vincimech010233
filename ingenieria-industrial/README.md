## Published technical work

- [OEE Downtime Analyzer](https://github.com/vincimech010233/oee-downtime-analyzer) — analyzes a fully synthetic shift CSV for Availability, Performance, Quality, OEE and downtime Pareto. Its hand-checkable tests and reproducible report illustrate manufacturing operations analysis; no real plant or MES/SCADA data was validated.
- [Tolerance Stack-up Capability](https://github.com/vincimech010233/tolerance-stackup-capability) — separate dimensional and mechanical quality analysis using hypothetical clearances, tolerance stack-up and capability metrics. It does not measure production OEE.
- [ThermoGap](https://github.com/vincimech010233/thermogap) — research-stage study of using residual heat to support an additional product at the same factory. Includes evidence review, synthetic cases, and a critical review; no production software or industrial validation.

These projects address different engineering questions. OEE depends on the stated time, cycle and quality definitions; the sample rankings are not operational recommendations for a real plant.
