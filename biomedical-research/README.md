# Biomedical research notes

[Español](README.es.md)

This section collects exploratory work in biomedical data and protein-design feasibility. These projects are research records, not clinical products or validated biological findings.

- [Blood RNA data feasibility for early lung adenocarcinoma research](https://github.com/vincimech010233/lung-adenocarcinoma-data-feasibility) — public-cohort metadata review; no diagnostic model or clinical validation.
- [EGFR minibinder feasibility study](https://github.com/vincimech010233/egfr-minibinder-feasibility-study) — sequence/structure preparation; model attempt did not produce candidates.

Both projects state what was checked, what was not verified, and what evidence would be needed next. No patient data are published.
